Blue Zones: The Path To 100

What are Blue Zones ?

Blue Zones are five geographic regions where people tend to live significantly longer and healthier lives than the global average: Okinawa (Japan), Sardinia (Italy), Nicoya Peninsula (Costa Rica), Ikaria (Greece), and Loma Linda (USA).

Researchers studying these areas identified nine lifestyle principles (often referred to as the Blue Zone "Power 9") that appear to contribute to this exceptional longevity. These principles include four spiritually oriented pillars and five related to daily habits, such as natural movement (walking, gardening etc.), stress reduction, the 80% Rule (stopping eating before feeling full), a predominantly plant-based diet, and moderate wine consumption.

Our Approach

What is the goal of this website?

In a society obsessed with finding unnatural ways to extend life, Blue Zones stand out as geographical anomalies where people naturally attain extraordinary longevity. While the nine pillars explain how these centenarians live, we asked ourselves a broader question: are these optimal conditions met in other places around the world?

"We decided to create a Blue Zone Index to identify Turquoise Countries, places that closely mirror these longevity conditions."

Translating qualitative lifestyle habits into global quantitative data is a complex challenge. Among the 9 original pillars, we isolated 5 key indicators that have reliable, measurable data worldwide.

When direct measurements were impossible, we carefully selected statistical proxies. For instance, the pillar of "downshifting" (stress reduction) has been modeled using national happiness levels, allowing us to accurately map this crucial mindset across the globe.
