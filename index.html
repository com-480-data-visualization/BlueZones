The 5 Blue Zones

Click a card to learn more. Hover to pause the carousel.

Okinawa

Fun Facts

  • Contains Japan's Shortest River.
  • Karate is an Okinawan export.
  • 36% of Okinawa's Land is Protected.
  • Their salt is in the Guinness Book of World Records.
Ikaria

Fun Facts

  • Named after Icarus, the Greek mythological figure.
  • Goats roam everywhere and outperform people.
  • Strong Greek Orthodox traditions shape community life.
  • Traditional "panigiria" (village festivals) are central to life.
Sardinia

Fun Facts

  • It is the second largest island in the Mediterranean.
  • They have a distinct language (Sardinian).
  • It has one of Europe's deepest caves.
  • Sardinia's coastline measures around 1850km long.
Nicoya Peninsula

Fun Facts

  • Surf and Yoga culture.
  • Near permanent sunshine.
  • Its parks have some of the rarest animals in the world.
  • Almost 80 miles of beach along the Pacific Coast.
Loma Linda

Fun Facts

  • It is known for its vegan cuisine.
  • It is home to a number of hiking trails.
  • It's University is a leading institution in health research.
  • It has a Mediterranean climate.
